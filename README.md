### 1. [Interviewer Viva Skill](https://github.com/Vinit-source/Skills/tree/interviewer-viva-skill/viva):
##### Interviewer skill prepared during PhD interview preparation for IITs
This skill helped me a lot to get a compounded support during my interview preparation for PhD and IITs.

It features:
- concept revision 
- exploring missing links in your understanding
-  very granular feedback on your answers
- #Vivek: Supportive real-time assistant that comes to rescue and adapts to your level of understanding with analogies from your background and previously answered confident questions
- Effective grouping of topics and formulation of questions
- Progress report and formula list maintained alongside.

Use it with Claude/Gemini/ChatGPT/any other skill-based agent. Upload the viva folder or viva.zip (You need to zip it).
